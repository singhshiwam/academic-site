    Day by day

      These are model values for the grid cell around you, not station measurements, and the later days are less certain than the first two. The shaded band on the temperature chart, when it appears, is the spread of Google's WeatherNext 2 ensemble, a coarser global model. Pollutant bars mark the WHO 2021 guideline for a 24-hour mean (8 hours for ozone) against the current hourly value, so read them as a rough guide. Advisories are worked out automatically with fixed thresholds: IMD rainfall classes, Beaufort wind force, and the US air quality index.
